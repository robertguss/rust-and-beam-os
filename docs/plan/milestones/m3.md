## Outcome

Cross the central feasibility gate by booting unchanged upstream ERTS inside the custom OS with real SMP behavior.

## Scope

* Load static `beam.smp` and reach its C entry point.
* Complete ERTS initialization and boot `kernel` and `stdlib`.
* Load modules from the read-only release tree.
* Exercise Erlang processes, messages, timers, ETS, binaries, and garbage collection.
* Prove two normal BEAM schedulers plus required dirty/async/auxiliary native threads.
* Capture thread, mapping, syscall, crash, QMP, and GDB evidence.

## Exit criteria

* Upstream ERTS boots successfully 100 consecutive times.
* A 12-hour runtime stress run completes without deadlock, kernel panic, ERTS abort, unknown syscall, or violation of the pre-frozen memory-slope/confidence and exact-resource rules.
* Two normal schedulers execute work concurrently on four vCPUs.
* 10,000 Erlang processes create, communicate, and terminate within budget.
* Repeated timers and forced GCs behave correctly.
* The upstream-diff budget passes.
* GATE-3 records whether the core Rust-kernel/BEAM hypothesis is proven.

## Implementation-readiness status — 2026-08-30

**Gate-blocked; not authorized.** Concurrency requires barrier-synchronized overlapping CPU work correlated across ERTS scheduler, OS thread, and vCPU identity. Boot/process/stress qualification uses the pre-frozen runner, progress, exact-resource, warm-up, slope, confidence, invalid-run, retry, and first-failure rules.
